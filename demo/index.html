Reactive Omni · Lasna + Base Sepolia

Triage

Leave a clerk watching your approvals. Origin logs are scored on Lasna, opened as cases on Base Sepolia, and judged by an in-loop policy agent.

The loop

  1. 01
    Origin event
    A Transfer or Approval is emitted by the watched token on Base Sepolia.
  2. 02
    react() on Lasna
    TriageReactive decodes the log, assigns a score, and requests an Omni callback. Ordinary mismatches return without reverting.
  3. 03
    openCase on the desk
    The official callback proxy injects the reactive address. TriageDesk records the case.
  4. 04
    Policy verdict
    The agent files record, watch, or escalate. Unlimited approvals raise AlertRaised.
